About EYSA

Study deeply.
Learn across cultures.

The East Asian Studies Youth Association is a student-led club creating space for young people to ask thoughtful questions about East Asia. Through collaborative research and writing, we make complex topics approachable and invite more voices into the conversation.

A note from our founder LN

Meet Lia Nan

“I’m Lia Nan, founder of EYSA. I was born in Japan, come from a Korean-Chinese background, and attend an IB international school. I started EYSA to bring young people together to explore East Asian studies through research and writing.”
